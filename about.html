Orbit Studio is my small web design project for local businesses. I am learning to make websites that explain what a business offers and make it easy to get in touch.

01 / WHY ORBITMADE FOR THE INDEPENDENT

A good website
starts with listening.

A website should help people understand what you do, feel good about choosing you, and know what to do next.

I start by asking what customers need to know first. Those answers shape the pages and help keep the site focused on the actual business.

Then I put the words, photos, and useful details in order. The aim is a site that feels familiar to its owner and is easy for a new customer to use.
